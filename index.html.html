We’re Dedicated To You

Manorama Super Speciality Hospital is Nizamabad’s first and largest super speciality hospital. We have full-time doctors, so someone is always here when you need help. Our pharmacy, lab, and emergency services are open 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

We treat every patient like family. Our goal is simple — give you the best treatment and send you home healthy.
